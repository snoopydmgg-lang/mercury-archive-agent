## Rubric Derived from Benchmark

Based on 258 samples across 9 accounts:

### High-performance correlates
- **ER (情感共鸣) high**: Top videos all have strong emotional anchoring
- **HP (钩子强度) high**: Counter-intuitive or curiosity-gap openings dominate
- **AB (受众广度) high**: Broad-appeal products outperform niche

### Low-performance correlates
- **SR (社会议题共振) not significant**: Not a factor for book/art content
- **SAT (讽刺深度) not significant**: Not applicable

### Recommendation
- ER and HP should be weighted higher for this niche
- Consider adding "产品叙事力" dimension during first bump
- Wait for 5+ of our own videos before adjusting rubric weights
